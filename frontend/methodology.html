How CRYPTO.SHIELD Works

Understand ingestion, graph features, ML, anomaly detection, scoring and the synthetic-demo policy.

Ingest

Chunked CSV ingestion and JSON/XML inspection with schema coverage reporting.

Detect

Graph-derived features, supervised baseline and Isolation Forest anomaly detection.

Explain

Risk prioritizes investigation; it is not proof of criminal activity. Every alert exposes its contributing evidence, source feature and supporting metric. Missing fields are explicitly unavailable rather than guessed.

⚠ Synthetic Data Policy

Synthetic enrichment is demonstration-only. It is deterministic, clearly labeled, used only for missing fields when enabled, and never overwrites user-supplied values.
